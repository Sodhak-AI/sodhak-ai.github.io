Overview

Every prompt, tool call and model request across your company, live. See which AI tools people use, how much, and where findings are coming from.

    app.sodhak.ai

    शोधक

    sodhak, Sanskrit: one who refines, purifies, searches out.

    AI is now part of how work gets done. What goes into it needs a refiner.

    Every prompt is a small data transfer to a company you don't control. Most are harmless. Some carry a production key, a customer's PAN, or an instruction an attacker planted in a document. SODHAK checks each one on the way through, so people keep using AI and the risky parts never leave.

    1. See

      Find every AI tool, model and agent in use: cloud accounts, browsers, laptops, coding agents and your own apps.

    2. Check

      Each prompt, response and tool call is checked for secrets, personal data, source code, prompt injection and jailbreaks.

    3. Act

      Allow it, redact it, block it, or ask the person why. The right team hears about it in Slack or your SIEM.

    4. Prove

      A searchable record of who used which AI, with what data, ready for ISO 27001, SOC 2 and DPDP Act reviews.

    Try it on a prompt.

    Edit the text and see what SODHAK would catch. This runs in your browser; nothing is sent anywhere.
